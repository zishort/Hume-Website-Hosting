HESS  
Electro-Magnetic Locks  
A new generation of low voltage electronic locking systems with the ultimate aim of providing positive solutions to life safety and security

Features

  • Easy installation
  • Self-contained unit
  • Low energy consumptiuon
  • Silent, continuous duty operation
  • Applicable to all single and pairs of swinging, sliding and roll-up doors
  • Engineering for system compatibility with virtually all electric locking devices for both interior and exterior applications
  • All controls can be aligned into the fire alarm system to ensure instaneous release of all locking devices in the event of fire.
  • Holding force of 300 / 600 / 1200 lbs upwards will effectively stop intruders and thieves from either side of the doors.
